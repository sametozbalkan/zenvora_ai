Hepatitis B remains a major problem for public health worldwide and represents a 
challenging disease for practicing physicians. Of the 2 billion people who have 
been infected with the hepatitis B virus, more than 350 million have chronic 
infections. These chronically infected individuals are at high risk of death 
from cirrhosis and liver cancer. The use of new antiviral drugs, such us 
nucleotides analogues, offer good hope in the prognosis of patients suffering 
from chronic hepatitis B.
